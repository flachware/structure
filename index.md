# On the Structure of Type

In considering the nature of the letter, its information structure may be approached in this way: A letter is a word from another alphabet. More specifically, a letter is a word from a *spatial alphabet*, the symbols of which are spatial points.

Figure: spatial word – point at top center, point at right center, point at bottom center, point at left center, point at top center

…

To make explicit the structure of the spatial word, we may translate its alphabet into a mathematical alphabet. The spatial word is thereby represented as a relation between points:
